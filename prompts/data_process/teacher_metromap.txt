# Authoritative Calculation

For a candidate route `v1, v2, ..., vn`, score the start station and all intermediate stations, and exclude the destination. Let `N=n-1` be the number of scored stations.

Compute:

`T = (sum of Time + sum of Transfer Time at actual line changes) / 3`

`P = sum of Price / 1.5`

`C = sum of (1 - Comfort Level) / N`

`R = sum of (1 - Reliability) / N`

`RouteScore = w1*T + w2*P + w3*C + w4*R`

All values, including zero, participate normally. Add a station's Transfer Time exactly once when the active metro line changes there. Passing through a multi-line station on the same active line adds no transfer cost. The legal candidate with the minimum RouteScore is the final route.
